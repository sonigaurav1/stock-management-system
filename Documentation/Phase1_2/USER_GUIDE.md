- **Revenue report** - How much you earned this month

- **Tooltips** - Click (?) on any metric for plain English explanation
- **Smart tips** - Suggestions to improve your business

## Getting Started (10 Minutes)

### Step 1: Run the Setup Wizard (3 min)

1. Click the **"📋 Setup Guide"** button
2. Follow 5 simple steps:
   - ✔️ Add your top 5-10 products
   - ✔️ Add your main suppliers
   - ✔️ Customize which metrics you see
   - ✔️ Record your first sale
   - ✔️ Explore your first reports

**Tip:** Each step takes 1-3 minutes. Don't skip this!

### Step 2: Understand the Three Key Metrics (3 min)

**Metric 1: Profit Margin %**

- What it means: For every ₹100 you sell, how many rupees do you keep?
- Where to find it: P&L tab, "Gross Profit" card
- What's healthy: 20-30% is good, 15% is minimum
- What to do if low: Raise prices or reduce costs

**Metric 2: Cash Position**

- What it means: How much money do you have right now?
- Where to find it: Cash Flow tab, top card (green/amber/red)
- What's healthy: Should be positive and 2-3 weeks of expenses
- What to do if low: Collect money from customers faster

**Metric 3: Outstanding Receivables**

- What it means: How much money customers still owe you?
- Where to find it: Cash Flow tab, "Customer Receivables"
- What's healthy: Less than 30% of monthly sales
- What to do if high: Follow up on late payments

### Step 3: Check Your First Report (4 min)

1. Click **"P&L"** tab
2. Select **"Monthly"** period
3. Look at the three colored boxes:
   - Blue = Revenue (total sales)
   - Amber = Cost (what you paid for products)
   - Green = Profit (what you keep)
4. Check the percentage - is it 20% or more?
5. Read the "What This Means" section for suggestions

## Key Features Explained

### Feature 1: Profit & Loss Report

**Where to find it:** Dashboard → P&L tab

**What you'll see:**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Revenue: ₹100,000                       │ <- Money you earned
│ Cost of Goods: ₹75,000                  │ <- What you paid for products
│ ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━     │
│ Gross Profit: ₹25,000 (25% margin)      │ <- What you keep
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Why it matters:**

- Shows if your business is actually profitable
- Helps you find products to stop selling (low margins)
- Lets you set better prices
- Shows if you're trending up or down

**How to use it:**

1. Check monthly P&L every month (set a calendar reminder!)
2. Look for red flags in "Low Margin Products" tab
3. Compare this month to last month - are you improving?
4. If margin is dropping, either raise prices or reduce costs

**Tips:**

- Your margin should be 20-30% (depends on your product type)
- If one product has negative margin, discontinue it or raise price
- Seasonal businesses may have variable margins

### Feature 2: Cash Flow Dashboard

**Where to find it:** Dashboard → Cash Flow tab

**What you'll see:**

**Cash Position Card** (Top left)

- Shows your estimated cash on hand
- Color: Green (healthy), Amber (warning), Red (critical)
- This is most important! Check it daily.

**Quick Metrics**

- Cash Received: Total money from customers
- Outstanding: Money customers still owe you

**Four Sub-dashboards:**

#### A) Payment Due Alerts

- Shows bills you need to pay to suppliers
- Red = Pay within 3 days (urgent!)
- Yellow = Pay within 7 days
- Blue = Normal (14+ days)
- **Action:** Pay urgent bills first, negotiate terms with others

#### B) Receivables (Money Customers Owe)

- Shows top customers who haven't paid yet
- Click customer to see details
- **Action:** Call top 3 to collect payment

#### C) Payables (Money You Owe Suppliers)

- Shows suppliers you haven't paid yet
- Shows payment history with each
- **Action:** Check if payment terms are negotiable

#### D) Cash Forecast

- Shows estimated cash for next 3 months
- Red line = warning, you may run out of cash
- **Action:** If forecast is negative, collect receivables or reduce spending

## Using the Dashboard Daily

### Morning Ritual (2 minutes)

1. Check **Cash Position** (Cash Flow tab, top card)
2. If red/amber: Check **Payment Alerts** to see what's due
3. If cash is low: Check **Receivables** to see who to collect from

### Weekly Review (10 minutes)

1. Check **P&L Report** (P&L tab)
2. Review **Top Products** - are they still profitable?
3. Check **Low Margin Products** warning
4. Note any products to discontinue

### Monthly Deep Dive (30 minutes)

1. Generate full **Monthly P&L Report**
2. Compare to last month - better or worse?
3. Review **Profit Trend** - 3-month trend
4. Review **Cash Flow Forecast** for next quarter
5. Plan actions: price changes, product mix changes, cost reductions

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: "My profit is down this month"

**What to do:**

1. Go to P&L tab
2. Check "Top Products by Profit" - are you selling different products?
3. Check "3-Month Trend" - is this a seasonal dip?
4. Check individual product margins - did you lower prices?
5. **Action:** Understand root cause and make pricing adjustments

### Scenario 2: "I'm running low on cash"

**What to do:**

1. Go to Cash Flow tab
2. Check **Cash Position** - how low is it?
3. Check **Receivables** - how much do customers owe?
4. Check **Payment Alerts** - what bills are due?
5. **Action:**
   - Immediately follow up on top receivables
   - Consider delaying non-urgent supplier payments (negotiate!)
   - Check if any quick sales can generate cash

### Scenario 3: "Which product should I stop selling?"

**What to do:**

1. Go to P&L tab
2. Click "⚠️ Low Margin" tab
3. Look for red products (negative margin) or very low margins
4. Check if the product is high volume (saves it) or low volume (skip it)
5. **Action:** Stop selling red products, unless they're bestsellers

### Scenario 4: "How do I know if my business is healthy?"

**What to do:**

1. Check **Cash Position** - is it positive and growing?
2. Check **P&L Margin** - is it 20% or higher?
3. Check **Outstanding Receivables** - less than 30% of revenue?
4. Check **3-Month Trend** - revenue trending up?
5. If all YES: Your business is healthy! 🎉

## Interpreting Your Numbers

### Profit Margin Benchmarks

```
< 10%   : 🔴 Danger zone - business is barely profitable
10-20%  : 🟡 Below average - need to act soon
20-30%  : 🟢 Healthy - good spot
30-50%  : 🟢 Excellent - keep doing what you're doing
> 50%   : 🟢 Premium - you have pricing power
```

### Cash Position Benchmarks

```
Negative  : 🔴 Emergency - you can't pay bills
0-2 weeks : 🟡 Warning - collect receivables urgently
2-4 weeks : 🟡 Okay - but room to improve
4-8 weeks : 🟢 Good - normal target
8+ weeks  : 🟢 Excellent - very healthy buffer
```

### Receivables Benchmarks

```
< 10% of sales : 🟢 Excellent - customers pay fast
10-20%         : 🟢 Good - normal for retail
20-40%         : 🟡 Concerning - customers slow to pay
> 40%          : 🔴 Problem - too much credit given
```

## Glossary (Plain English)

| Term                     | What It Means                                      |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Revenue**              | Total money you earned from sales                  |
| **COGS (Cost of Goods)** | What you paid to buy products you sold             |
| **Gross Profit**         | Revenue minus COGS (money left for other expenses) |
| **Margin %**             | Gross Profit as percentage of Revenue              |
| **Cash Position**        | Estimated money you have right now                 |
| **Receivables**          | Money customers owe you                            |
| **Payables**             | Money you owe suppliers                            |
| **Outstanding**          | Still unpaid/not received                          |
| **Aged Receivables**     | How old unpaid invoices are (30, 60, 90+ days)     |
| **Forecast**             | Prediction based on past trends                    |
| **Break-even**           | How many units needed to cover costs               |

## Tips & Best Practices

### ✅ Do This:

- Check cash position every day
- Review P&L every month
- Follow up on receivables within 5 days of due date
- Set reminder to review on 1st of each month
- Use knowledge base when confused
- Compare month-to-month, not day-to-day (too noisy)

### ❌ Don't Do This:

- Don't just look at revenue (profit is what matters!)
- Don't ignore low margin products (they drag down profit)
- Don't wait for "someday" to collect receivables
- Don't let cash position go negative (very hard to recover)
- Don't make big decisions based on one day/week of data

## FAQ

**Q: How often should I check the dashboard?**
A: Daily check cash position (2 min), weekly check P&L (10 min), monthly deep dive (30 min)

**Q: Why is my margin low?**
A: Either your selling price is too low or cost is too high. Negotiate with suppliers or raise prices.

**Q: How do I improve profit?**
A: (1) Raise prices on bestsellers, (2) Reduce costs via supplier negotiation, (3) Stop selling low-margin products

**Q: What's a good cash position?**
A: 4-8 weeks of expenses. Less than 2 weeks is risky.

**Q: How do I get customers to pay faster?**
A: (1) Send invoice same day, (2) Set clear terms (due in 15 days), (3) Call on day 20 if unpaid, (4) Offer discount for early payment

**Q: The numbers seem wrong. What do I do?**
A: Check that you've recorded all sales correctly. Look at raw data in Sales section. Contact support if still confused.

**Q: Can I export these reports?**
A: Coming in next phase! For now, you can take screenshots or talk to support.

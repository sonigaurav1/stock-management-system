## Key Business Metrics You'll Understand

### Sales Metrics

- **Total Revenue**: All sales combined
- **Revenue by Category**: Which categories drive sales
- **Revenue by Product**: Top and bottom performers
- **Revenue Trend**: Is business growing or declining
- **Revenue vs Target**: How close are you to goals

### Profitability Metrics

- **Gross Profit**: Revenue minus cost of goods sold
- **Gross Margin**: Profit as percentage of revenue (higher is better)
- **Net Profit**: Profit after expenses
- **Product Margin**: Which products are most profitable
- **Break-Even Analysis**: How many units to sell to break even

### Inventory Metrics

- **Stock Value**: Total money tied up in inventory
- **Inventory Turnover**: How many times inventory sells per year (higher is better)
- **Dead Stock**: Products not selling (opportunity to remove)
- **Reorder Needs**: Which products to order now
- **Stockout Risk**: Products at risk of running out

### Customer Metrics

- **New Customers**: How many new customers acquired
- **Customer Lifetime Value**: Average revenue per customer over lifetime
- **Best Customers**: Who are your top 20% customers (that drive 80% of profit)
- **Churn Risk**: Customers likely to stop buying
- **Purchase Frequency**: How often customers buy

### Cash Flow Metrics

- **Cash on Hand**: How much money you have
- **Payables**: Money owed to suppliers (due soon)
- **Receivables**: Money owed by customers
- **Cash Forecast**: Will you have enough cash next month
- **Payment Schedule**: When money is coming in/going out

## Understanding Your Dashboard

### Dashboard Components

**1. Key Metrics Cards** (Top Section)

- Shows 4-6 most important numbers for your role
- Green/Red indicators show if trending well
- Click any card to drill deeper

**2. Sales Trend Chart** (Main Section)

- Last 12 months of sales
- See seasonality and growth
- Compare to same period last year

**3. Product Performance Table**

- Top 10 products by revenue
- Bottom 10 products (good to remove?)
- Profit margin for each product

**4. Cash Flow Forecast**

- Next 6 months cash position
- Green if positive, red if negative cash expected
- Helps plan for cash crunches

**5. Alerts Section** (Bottom)

- Products running low on stock
- Customers not paying on time
- Unusual spikes/drops in sales

**6. AI Insights Panel**

- Recommendations: "Consider stocking more of X, it's trending"
- Anomalies: "Sales are down 15% vs last week, here's why"
- Actions: "Reorder for 3 products to meet forecast demand"

## Example Business Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Retail Store Owner

**Situation**: You want to know if your business is healthy and where to focus.

**Solution**:

1. Open dashboard → See revenue trending down 10%
2. Click AI Insights → "Your electronics category sales dropped 25%"
3. Drill into electronics → "Phone cases dropped, but chargers up 5%"
4. Action: Reduce phone case inventory, increase chargers
5. Result: Inventory optimized, working capital freed up

### Scenario 2: Multi-Location Manager

**Situation**: Managing 3 stores, want to see performance and compare.

**Solution**:

1. Open dashboard → Filter by location
2. Store A: Revenue up 20%, Store B: down 5%, Store C: flat
3. Check Store B details → "Inventory turnover down, too much dead stock"
4. Action: Reduce stock levels in Store B, reallocate to Store A
5. Result: Consistent performance across stores

### Scenario 3: Finance Manager - Month-End

**Situation**: It's month-end, need P&L, cash flow, and compliance reports.

**Solution**:

1. Go to Reports → Select "Monthly P&L"
2. System generates automatically with all details
3. Check audit trail → Verify all transactions are recorded
4. Check cash flow forecast → Plan for next month's payments
5. Export to Excel → Send to accountant/owner
6. Result: Reports ready in 15 minutes instead of 2-3 hours

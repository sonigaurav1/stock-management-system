## 🚀 Key Features at a Glance

### 1. Smart Dashboard

Dashboard automatically displays KPIs relevant to your business:

- **Retail**: Top products, inventory, sales trends, customer metrics
- **Wholesale**: Sales volume, supplier performance, reorder needs
- **Manufacturing**: Production efficiency, material costs, supplier metrics
- **Distribution**: Inventory turnover, delivery metrics, sales trends
- **Services**: Revenue, utilization, client metrics, cash flow

### 2. One-Click Insights 💡

Automatic alerts when important things happen:

- ⚠️ **Sales Anomaly**: "Sales down 15% vs last week"
- 📦 **Low Stock**: "5 products need reordering"
- 💰 **Payment Due**: "You have $5,000 in unpaid invoices"
- ✅ **Growth**: "Best month - sales up 25%!"

### 3. Customize Your Dashboard

Arrange exactly how you want:

1. Click **"Customize"** button
2. Drag widgets to reorder
3. Toggle visibility on/off
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### 4. Live Updates 🔄

Dashboard automatically updates every 30 seconds. Click **"Refresh"** for instant updates.

### 5. Export Reports 📥

Download dashboard for sharing:

1. Click **"Export"** button
2. Choose: PDF, Excel, or CSV
3. Select widgets to include
4. Choose date range
5. Download

Perfect for:

- Investor meetings
- Monthly records

- Team discussions

### 6. Mobile Support 📱

Full dashboard on your phone - all features work on any device.

## 📊 Understanding Your Dashboard

### Revenue Widget

- **Large number**: Total revenue this month
- **% below**: Change from last month
  - 🔴 Red (negative) = Sales decreased
  - 🟢 Green (positive) = Sales increased

### Sales Count

- **Number**: How many transactions this month
- **%**: Change vs previous month

### Customer Count

- **Number**: Active customers this month
- **%**: Change vs previous month

### Inventory Health

- 🟢 **Green**: Healthy stock levels
- 🟡 **Yellow**: Low stock - reorder soon
- 🔴 **Red**: Out of stock

### Cash Flow

- Shows money owed to you (receivables)
- Shows money you owe (payables)

## 🎯 Common Tasks

### Customize Your Dashboard

1. **Click "Customize"** in top right
2. **Drag widgets** to change order (grab the dots on left)
3. **Toggle switch** to show/hide widgets
4. **Click "Save Changes"** when done

Your layout is automatically saved!

### Investigate an Alert

1. **Click the alert card** with warning
2. **Click "View Products"** or action link
3. **Take action** (reorder, follow up on payment, etc.)
4. **Click X** to dismiss alert

### Export Your Dashboard

1. **Click "Export"** in top right
2. **Choose format**:
   - PDF: Professional report with charts
   - Excel: Spreadsheet for analysis
   - CSV: Data for imports
3. **Select widgets** to include (or all)
4. **Choose date range**: Today / Last 7 days / Last 30 days
5. **Click "Export"** and wait for download

### Check on Mobile

- Open dashboard on your phone
- Widgets automatically stack in single column
- All features work exactly the same
- Perfect for checking during shifts

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why did I get a low stock alert?**  
A: When stock drops below 25% of your reorder level, the system alerts you so you have time to reorder before running out.

**Q: Can I change when I get alerts?**  
A: Yes! In settings, you can adjust alert thresholds for your business type.

**Q: Will my customized dashboard stay the same?**  
A: Yes! Your layout is saved automatically.

**Q: Can I go back to default layout?**  
A: Yes! In customize menu, click "Reset to Defaults".

**Q: What if I misread a metric?**  
A: Each metric has a tooltip explaining what it means. Hover over titles for more info.

**Q: Can I share my dashboard?**  
A: Yes! Export to PDF and share. Or keep it private - your choice.

**Q: Does it work offline?**  
A: Dashboard will show your last cached data while offline, then update when back online.

**Q: Can I export just certain widgets?**  
A: Yes! When exporting, select which widgets to include.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Dashboard doesn't load

- Refresh page (⌘R or Ctrl+R)
- Check internet connection
- Try different browser (Chrome recommended)

### Insights not showing

- You need sales data first
- System learns patterns over 1-2 weeks
- Check if thresholds make sense for your business

### Widgets look weird on mobile

- This is normal - they adjust to phone screen
- Rotate phone to landscape for more space
- Try on tablet for better experience

### Export not working

- Try PDF format first
- Select at least one widget
- Check your date range is valid
- Try again in a moment
